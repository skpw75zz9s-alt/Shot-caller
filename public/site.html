How it works

Kalshi runs a new bitcoin round every 15 minutes: will BTC close above the target price or below it?

It watches

BTC's price on 12 exchanges, the order flow, volatility and Kalshi's own prices, every second.

It calls

When one side's odds clear the bar (and Kalshi's price agrees), you get an UP or DOWN call with its price and confidence. Most rounds get no call.

It grades itself

Every call is checked against the round's result. Learners only influence calls after proving themselves on data they never saw.

Questions

Is it guaranteed to win?

No. Every call can lose, and some will. The record above shows both, and the app shows profit after fees so a high win rate can't hide losses.

Is Shot Caller part of Kalshi?

No. Shot Caller is an independent app. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Kalshi. It reads Kalshi's public market data.

Does it trade for me?

Not unless you set it up. The optional Auto-trader is off by default; it can place orders only with your own Kalshi API keys and limits you choose. Otherwise you place your own trades on Kalshi.

Who can use it?

Kalshi requires users to be 18 or older, and availability depends on where you live. Check Kalshi's terms for your state.

How do I get access?

Tap Get access, pay, and your access is approved by hand, usually within a few hours. Already paid on another device? Use Sign in and enter your code.

Is there a community?

Yes: the official Shot Caller Discord, where every call and result is posted automatically. Join the Discord.
